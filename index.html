Decentralized Onion Routing Layer

The Dark-Net Locator framework leverages decentralized overlay networks where data transmission packets are wrapped in multiple layers of encryption, mimicking an onion structure[cite: 1]. Each intermediary node (Entry Guard, Middle Relays, and Exit Nodes) only possesses cryptographic keys sufficient to decrypt its specific routing layer[cite: 1].

By ensuring that no single node ever possesses knowledge of both the originating IP address and the final destination simultaneously, the protocol achieves absolute source-destination decoupling and protects operators from passive traffic correlation analysis[cite: 1].
